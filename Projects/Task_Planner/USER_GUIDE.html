⛩️ 1. The Kaizen Philosophy

Kaizen (改善) is the Japanese philosophy of continuous, incremental improvement. Instead of trying to radically transform everything overnight, Kaizen is built around getting 1% better every single day through disciplined, focused execution.

💡 The 1% Rule of Compounding Consistency

If you improve by just 1% every day for a year: (1.01)³⁶⁵ = 37.78x. You become nearly 38 times better by the time your target exam or major life goal arrives.

Kaizen combines long-term strategic roadmaps with high-focus daily task execution across three temporal dimensions: Day (Deep Focus), Week (Priority Sprints), and Month (High-Level Targets).

🚀 2. Comprehensive Feature Walkthrough

📈 90-Day Study Heatmap & Streaks

Visual contribution grid showing 90 days of study momentum. Tracks your active streak, all-time best streak, and total focus hours logged. Click any square to jump directly to that date.

🍅 Customizable Pomodoro Timer

Configurable deep work timer (5–90m focus, short/long breaks). Includes zero-dependency synthesizer bell chime and dynamic tab countdown title (24:45) Kaizen.

⏰ Due Times & Push Reminders

Type @10:30am or @15:00 in any task to create an automatic time reminder. Background scanner fires native browser notifications when tasks are due.

🧠 Spaced Repetition Scheduler

Click "Spaced Revision" on any subject or chapter to automatically schedule revision milestones on Day +1, Day +3, and Day +7.

🏷️ Subject Hashtags & Color Filtering

Type hashtags like #Physics, #Math, #Revision to auto-badge tasks with pastel tags. Click any tag to instantly filter your agenda.

📋 Master Explorer & Archive

Search across days, weeks, months, or years. Live multi-field search scans task titles, subtask checklists, and full study notes with 0ms latency.

📝 3. Task Descriptions, Notes & Checklists

Click the expand arrow (▼) on any task to unlock its full depth:

  • Study Notes & Formulas: Add multi-line notes, formulas, reference URLs, or chapter summaries. Notes auto-save continuously to local storage.
  • Subtask / Checklist Items: Break complex topics into discrete checklist items (e.g. "Review Chapter 4" $\rightarrow$ "Read theory", "Solve 15 numericals").
  • 1-Click Task Pomodoro: Tap 🍅 Focus inside any task to dedicate a Pomodoro focus block directly to that specific topic.

📱 4. Mobile Gestures & PWA Installation

👆 Touch Swipe Gestures

Swipe left or right anywhere on your phone screen to seamlessly flip dates:

  • Swipe Left (👈): Moves forward to Tomorrow / Next Week / Next Month.
  • Swipe Right (👉): Moves backward to Yesterday / Previous Week / Previous Month.

📳 Haptic Tactile Feedback

Provides subtle physical vibration clicks (navigator.vibrate) on Android and mobile devices whenever you complete tasks, check subtasks, or finish timers.

🎙️ Voice-to-Task Input

Tap the microphone button $\rightarrow$ speak your task $\rightarrow$ Kaizen automatically transcribes and adds the task hands-free using the Web Speech API.

📲 Installing the Standalone PWA (100% Offline)

  1. iPhone / iPad (iOS): Open in Safari $\rightarrow$ Tap Share $\rightarrow$ Tap Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: Open in Chrome $\rightarrow$ Tap the three dots (⋮) $\rightarrow$ Tap Install App or Add to Home Screen.
  3. Desktop (Chrome / Edge): Click the Install icon in the address bar.

🛡️ 5. Bulletproof 5-Tier Data Persistence

Kaizen is engineered so that you never lose tasks or study plans, even if you clear browser cookies or lose internet connection:

Tier Storage Mechanism Protection Level
Tier 1 localStorage + sessionStorage (Dual-Key) Instant synchronous client-side caching.
Tier 2 IndexedDB (TaskPlannerDB) Binary resilient storage immune to standard cookie wipes.
Tier 3 navigator.storage.persist() Requests OS-level browser disk protection against eviction.
Tier 4 Firebase Firestore Cloud Sync 2-way real-time multi-device sync via Google Auth or Secret Sync Passcode.
Tier 5 Pre-Unload Lifecycle Flush Auto-commits active input fields before page close or tab refresh.

📥 6. Export, Formatted Text & PDF Generation

Click "📥 Export & Download (TXT / PDF / JSON)" in the footer to access your download hub:

  • 📄 Formatted Text (.txt): Downloads a clean, human-readable text document with checkmarks ([✓] / [ ]), study notes, and checklists. Works seamlessly on Windows Notepad, Mac TextEdit, and mobile note apps.
  • 🖨️ Print / Save as PDF: Generates a dedicated high-resolution document in an isolated background frame containing 100% of task titles, study notes, checklists, and metadata on clean white paper with zero UI clutter.
  • 💾 JSON Backup & Restore: Full state backup with safety confirmation safeguards.
